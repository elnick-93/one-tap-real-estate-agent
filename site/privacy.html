Privacy Policy

We store agent emails, password hashes, API keys, and lead contact details you submit so the product can message those leads and show them in your CRM.

  • Lead data is scoped to the agent account that submitted it.
  • Twilio and SendGrid process messages you trigger.
  • Stripe processes subscription payments.
  • We do not sell lead lists.
  • Request deletion by emailing the operator who runs this instance.
